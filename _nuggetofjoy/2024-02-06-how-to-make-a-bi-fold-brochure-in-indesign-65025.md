What You'll Be Creating


In this quick and simple tutorial, you’ll learn how to make a bi-fold brochure template using Adobe InDesign. You’ll create the basic structure and style for your brochure template, using an on-trend color palette and stylish fonts.


![brochure mockup ](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/Mockup1.jpg)


![brochure mockup](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/Mockup2.jpg)


An adaptable pamphlet template for any creative business, this bi-fold brochure can be customized with your own graphics, photos, fonts and colors.


## What You’ll Need to Create Your Brochure


We’ll use Adobe InDesign to assemble the main artwork for the brochure. You’ll also need access to vector software, like Adobe Illustrator, to briefly open the icon illustrations if you choose to use them.


The fonts used in this brochure design are:


- [Adriatic Modern](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/1n944u1bpdeinqywpjwj2/Adriatic-Bold.ttf?rlkey=3gmxen7ymcvporkjwqwcvie24&dl=1)
- [Hamlin Minimal](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/j0kq51ngvwuv5esrxvzom/Hamlin.zip?rlkey=t246cp3e7iu0lfc0whfnis0h4&dl=1)


Download and install the fonts on your computer.


This set of creative vector icons is also used in the design pictured here.


You will also need to download this provided set of [Adobe Swatch Exchange (ASE) color swatches](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/attachment/Bifold%20Brochure%20Color%20Swatches.ase). We’ll look at how to load these into your InDesign document later.


## 1. How to Set Up the Basics of Your Brochure Template


### Step 1


Open **InDesign** and go to **File > New > Document.**


Choose **Print** from the options along the top of the window, and set the **Page Size** to **A4, Landscape.**


Set the number of **Pages** to **12** (or any number you prefer, you can always add or remove pages later), and keep **Facing Pages** checked.


Set the **Top** and **Outside Margins**to **18 mm**, and the **Inside** and **Bottom Margins** to a more generous **20 mm.**


Add a **Bleed** of **5 mm** to all edges of the page, except the Inside edge, which you can keep as**0 mm.**


![new document](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/1.1.jpg)


Click **Create**.


### Step 2


Expand the **Swatches** panel **(Window > Color > Swatches)**, and choose **Load Swatches**from the panel’s main menu.


![load swatches](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/1.2.jpg)


Navigate to the [ASE file](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/attachment/Bifold%20Brochure%20Color%20Swatches.ase) you downloaded earlier and click Open. The CMYK swatches will load into the panel and be ready for you to use on your brochure template.


![loaded swatches](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/1.2.1.jpg)


### Step 3


Expand the **Layers** panel **(Window > Layers)** and double-click on ***Layer 1***, renaming it ***Color***.


Create a second new layer and name this ***Type***.


![layers panel](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/1.3.jpg)


Lock the ***Type*** layer, as we’ll work on the ***Color*** layer first.


![color layer](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/1.3.1.jpg)


### Step 4


Expand the **Pages** panel **(Window > Pages)** and double-click the **A-Master** icon at the top to bring up the master spread on screen.


From the left-hand ruler **(View > Show Rulers)**, pull out a guide to **147.5 mm**, the center of the left-hand page.


![central guide](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/1.4.jpg)


Pull a second guide down from the top ruler to **104 mm**, to create a central cross across the page. These guides mark out the center-point of the page, which will help you to position content as you work on the brochure.


![guides](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/1.4.1.jpg)


Select both guides and **Edit > Copy**and **Edit > Paste** them, moving them over to the right page of the master.


![pasted guides](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/1.4.2.jpg)


## 2. How to Add Color to Your Brochure


### Step 1


Click on the **Page 1** icon in the **Pages** panel to bring up the page on screen. This will be the front cover of your brochure.


Working on the ***Color*** layer, use the **Rectangle Tool (M)** to create a shape across the bottom half of the page, extending it to the bleed on the right and bottom edges.


Set the **Fill Color**to a bright yellow swatch\* from the colors in the **Swatches** panel.


![yellow shape](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/2.1.jpg)


*\* You can vary the colors across your brochure design to give your layout a unique look.*


### Step 2


**Copy** the shape, and scroll down to the final page of your brochure (which will be the back cover). **Edit > Paste** the shape onto the page, and arrange it in the same position.


![yellow shape](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/2.2.jpg)


### Step 3


Scroll up to **Pages 2** and **3** of your document. This will be your contents page.


Create a new shape on the page using the **Rectangle Tool (M)**, extending it across the length of the whole spread and up to about two-thirds from the bottom of the spread.


Set the **Fill** to a pale pink swatch.


![pink rectangle](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/2.3.jpg)


### Step 4


Scroll down to the next spread, **Pages 4**and **5**. This will be the first main inside spread of your brochure.


Create a shape across **Page 4**, setting the **Fill** to match the cover’s color.


**Copy** and **Edit > Paste in Place**this shape onto the remaining spreads, varying the color between yellow and pink as you work.


![yellow color](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/2.4.jpg)


![color spread](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/2.4.1.jpg)


## 3. How to Format Type for the Front Cover of Your Brochure


### Step 1


Scroll up to **Page 1** of your document.


Lock the ***Color*** layer and unlock the top layer, ***Type***.


Use the**Type Tool (T)**to create a long text frame across the top-right of the page.


![type tool](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/3.1.jpg)


Type in the first part of the title of the brochure, and from either the top **Controls** panel or the **Character** and **Paragraph** panels **(Window > Type & Tables > Character / Paragraph)**, set the **Font** to **Adriatic Bold, Size 102 pt, Align Right**, and give the text a dark grey **Font Color**from the **Swatches** panel.


![title text frame](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/3.1.1.jpg)


### Step 2


**Copy** and **Paste** the text frame, position the copy below, and rest the text's baseline on the colored shape.


Set the **Font Color**to match the shape’s color, and edit the text to read the second part of the title.


![matching font color](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/3.2.jpg)


### Step 3


Add a subtitle text frame below set in **Hamlin ExtraBold**, a **40 pt Font Size**, and a **[Paper] Font Color.**


![subtitle text frame](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/3.3.jpg)


You can place blurb text below this in **Hamlin ExtraBold, Size 25 pt.**


![blurb text](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/3.3.1.jpg)


### Step 4


Set the business name in a small text frame to the bottom-left of the cover, setting this in **Hamlin ExtraBold, Size 15 pt.**


![business name](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/3.4.jpg)


Add an address below, set in a grey **Font Color.**


![business name address](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/3.4.1.jpg)


**Copy** and **Paste** both text frames, and move them to the right of the originals, editing the text to show your contact details.


![contact text frame](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/3.4.2.jpg)


## 4. How to Create a Contents Page for Your Brochure


### Step 1


Select and **Copy** one of the title text frames from the cover. Scroll down to **Page 3** and **Paste** onto the page, moving the frame to sit on top of the colored shape and adjusting the **Font Color**to match.


![font color match](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/4.1.jpg)


### Step 2


To create the list of contents, use the **Type Tool (T)** to create a small text frame in the center of **Page 3.**


With the baseline of the text on the central guide, type in the first chapter title. Set the **Font** to **Adriatic Bold, Size 22 pt,**and a **[Paper] Font Color.**


![chapter title](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/4.2.jpg)


### Step 3


Use the **Line Tool (\)** to create a long horizontal line from the right edge of the text’s baseline.


Expand the **Stroke** panel **(Window > Stroke)**and increase the **Weight** of the line to**3 pt.**Set the **Type** to **Dotted**.


![dotted line](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/4.3.jpg)


### Step 4


**Copy** and **Paste** the chapter name’s text frame and position it to the right of the dotted line. Edit the text to read the page number.


![page number](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/4.4.jpg)


Select both text frames and the line between them, and **Copy** and **Paste** them, moving this trio directly below.


![contents title](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/4.4.1.jpg)


Then continue to **Paste** more copies, spacing each line evenly.


![contents page](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/4.4.2.jpg)


### Step 5


Use the following link to find icons similar to the ones below:


Select one of the icons and **Edit > Copy** it.


![icons illustrator](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/4.5.jpg)


Head back to **InDesign**, and **Edit > Paste** the icon onto the left page of the contents spread.


You can directly select parts of the vector and adjust the color from the **Swatches** panel.


![vector icon](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/4.5.1.jpg)


Add more icons to the page to create a row effect.


![row of icons](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/4.5.2.jpg)


## 5. How to Format the Inside Pages of Your Brochure


### Step 1


Scroll down to **Pages 4**and **5** of your brochure. Working on **Page 5**, use the **Type Tool (T)**to create a large text frame on the right side of the page.


Type in the chapter number and set the **Font** to **Adriatic Light, Size 700 pt**. Switch the **Font Color**to match the color of the facing page.


![adriatic font](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.1.jpg)


### Step 2


Add a title text frame at the top-left of **Page 5**, and set the **Font** to **Adriatic Bold, Size 60 pt,**and a grey **Font Color.**


![title text frame](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.2.jpg)


Add a subtitle text frame in the same **Font**, sized at**32 pt.**


![subtitle frame](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.2.1.jpg)


Add body text below this, in two columns, set in **Hamlin Regular, 11 pt.**


![body text](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.2.2.jpg)


### Step 3


**Copy** over one of the icons from the Illustrator file and **Paste** this centrally onto the facing page.


![copied icon](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.3.jpg)


### Step 4


You can now use this spread as a basic template for the rest of the inside spreads.


Select and **Copy** all of the text frames on **Page 5.**


![copy elements](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.4.jpg)


Scroll down to the next spread and **Edit > Paste in Place.**


Adjust the **Font Color**of the chapter number to match, and **Paste** in another icon on the facing page.


![edited color](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.4.1.jpg)


![icon in center](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.4.2.jpg)


### Step 5


Continue to use this same process to populate the remaining inside spreads of your brochure template.


![spread three](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.5.jpg)


![spread four](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.5.1.jpg)


You could also replace the icons with photos for a different look to the layout. This might make your book more interesting.


![photo spread](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.5.2.jpg)


![photo spread](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/5.5.3.jpg)


## 6. How to Export Your Brochure for Printing


When you’ve finished working on your brochure, you’re ready to export it to a print-ready format. Most brochures look best when they’re printed and bound professionally, but to do this, you will have to provide the file as a PDF to the printer first.


### Step 1


Go to **File > Export**and choose **Adobe PDF (Print)** from the **Format** menu at the bottom of the window.


Name the file and click **Save**.


In the **Export** window that opens, choose **[Press Quality]** from the **Adobe PDF Preset**menu at the top. Make sure **Pages** is checked under the **Export As**option too.


![press quality](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/6.1.jpg)


### Step 2


Click on **Marks and Bleeds** in the window’s left-hand menu.


Check both **All Printer’s Marks**and **Use Document Bleed Settings**, before clicking **Export**.


![export pdf bleeds](https://cms-assets.tutsplus.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=850/uploads/users/114/posts/32479/image/6.2.jpg)


Awesome work! You can send this PDF straight off to be printed professionally at a print shop or online print-on-demand service.

Your brochure is finished—great job! You can easily add more pages to your design from the **Pages** panel, or switch up the color swatches and images for a different look.
