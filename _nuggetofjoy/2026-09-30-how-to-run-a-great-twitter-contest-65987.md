A good X contest or giveaway should be **simple, easy to understand, visually engaging, and designed as a campaign—not just one post.**


For this assignment, you will create an **8-post X contest campaign**. Each post should have a different purpose while maintaining a consistent visual style.


Before creating your campaign, review X's current contest guidelines


[https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-contest-rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-contest-rules%5B/embedpress)


# 1. Start With a Goal


First, decide what your contest is supposed to accomplish.


Possible goals include:


- Increase engagement
- Promote a product
- Build brand awareness
- Generate user-created content
- Increase followers
- Drive traffic to a website


### Example


**Goal:** Increase engagement with college-age sneaker fans while promoting a new sneaker.


The goal should influence everything else you create.


# 2. Know Your Audience


Be specific about who you are trying to reach.


**Too broad:**  
College students


**Better:**  
College students ages 18–24 who are interested in sneakers, fashion, and streetwear.


Your prize should also make sense for your audience.


### Example


**Contest:** Design Your Dream Sneaker


**Prize:** The winning sneaker design will actually be produced.


The prize gives the audience a reason to participate because it is directly connected to their interests.


# 3. Keep the Rules Simple


People should be able to understand how to enter almost immediately.


### Example


**HOW TO ENTER**


1. Create your sneaker design.
2. Post it on X.
3. Mention @YourBrand.
4. Use #DreamSneakerContest.


Avoid making the entry process unnecessarily complicated.


# 4. Create a Campaign — Not Just One Post


Your eight posts should work together as a campaign.


Don't simply make eight versions of the same advertisement.


Instead, give each post a different job:


**Launch → Prize → Instructions → Engagement → User Content → Behind the Scenes → Reminder → Final Call**


# Post 1: Launch


### Purpose


Introduce the contest and immediately communicate:


- What the contest is
- What the prize is
- How long people have to enter


### Example


**CONTEST TIME!**


Design your dream sneaker for a chance to have your design produced.


Enter by October 15.


#DreamSneakerContest

Blue + Orange  
Purple + Yellow

# Keep the Campaign Consistent


Although each post should have a different purpose, the campaign should look like it belongs together.


Define these before designing your posts:


- **Topic**
- **Goal**
- **Target audience**
- **Theme**
- **Voice/tone**
- **Color palette**
- **Typography**
- **Graphic style**
- **Hashtag**
- **Prize**
- **Contest dates**
- **Entry requirements**


### Example Campaign Style


**Campaign:** Dream Sneaker Contest


**Colors:** Black, white, neon green


**Tone:** Energetic and youthful


**Typography:** Bold sans-serif


**Graphic style:** High-contrast photography with large typography


**Hashtag:** #DreamSneakerContest


The eight posts should look like parts of the **same campaign**, not eight unrelated designs.


# Assignment


Create an **8-post X contest campaign**.


## Campaign Plan


Before designing your posts, define:


1. Topic
2. Goal
3. Target audience
4. Theme
5. Voice/tone
6. Color palette
7. Typography
8. Graphic style
9. Hashtag
10. Prize
11. Contest dates
12. Entry requirements


## Create 8 Posts


Your campaign must include:


1. **Launch**
2. **Prize Spotlight**
3. **How to Enter**
4. **Engagement**
5. **User-Generated Content**
6. **Behind the Scenes**
7. **Reminder**
8. **Final Call**


### Format Requirements


- **6 static/image-based posts**
- **2 video-based posts**
- Each post should have a different purpose.
- The campaign should have a consistent visual style.
- Entry requirements should be clear.
- The campaign should have a clear beginning, middle, and end.


## Final Submission


Before submitting, check that:


- All 8 posts are included.
- 6 posts are static/image-based.
- 2 posts are video-based.
- The visual style is consistent.
- Each post has a different purpose.
- The contest rules are easy to understand.
- The prize is clearly communicated.
- The beginning, middle, and end of the campaign are clear.
