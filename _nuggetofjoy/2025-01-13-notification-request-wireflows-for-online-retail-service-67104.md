## Design Scenario


You're a UX designer for a large international retail company. Your team aims to re-engage former users and keep current ones interested by sending notifications about offers, promotions, and other customer deals.


## Design Task


Your design task is to complete the following:


- Select a retail platform and the device type for your wirerflow.
- Design a wireflow to prompt users to enable notifications.
- Ensure the wireflow communicates the benefits of enabling notifications.
- Develop a wireflow showing how users can activate notifications if they initially declined.
- Explain the logic behind your wireflows.
- Use the provided mobile or desktop templates to showcase your designs.
- Bonus points for making your project submission interactive.🏆

## Evaluation criteria


Your project will be evaluated using the following criteria:


- **Interactivity** — How effectively does the interface facilitate user actions and provide meaningful feedback in response to those actions?
- **Usability** — Can users perform tasks safely, effectively, and efficiently while enjoying the experience?
- **Clarity** — Does the copy communicate the intended message clearly and effectively?
- **Presentation** — How effectively is the work presented, including the templates provided, the copy formatting, the links shared, and the explanation of the design rationale?
