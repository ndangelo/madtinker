## Design Scenario


You're a member of the design team at a video streaming service. Your task is to brainstorm ideas and design wireframes that offer a unique approach to service usability, setting it apart from competitors.


## Design Task


Your design task is to complete the following:


- Select a video streaming service and the device type for your design.
- Brainstorm ideas on how to enhance a video streaming service.
- Develop interactive wireframes for the most compelling ideas, choosing the level of fidelity you prefer.
- Explain the rationale behind your wireframes.
- Use the provided mobile and desktop templates to showcase your designs.
- Bonus points for conducting a competitive analysis, identifying the strengths and weaknesses of competitors. 🏆


## Instructions


Read the instructions to ensure your submission receives maximum visibility.


- You can edit your project at any time.
- Remember to share your submission with your community. The more reviews and views it receives, the quicker it will be seen by Uxcel Mentors.
- After submitting your project, consider reviewing others' projects. This can enhance your project's discoverability.
- Ensure you only submit your own work.


## Evaluation criteria


Your project will be evaluated using the following criteria:


- **Usability** — Can users perform tasks safely, effectively, and efficiently while enjoying the experience?
- **Creativity** — Does it solve problems and communicate ideas in an innovative way?
- **Feasibility** — How practical and realistic a proposed design solution is within the potential constraints of a project?
- **Visual design** — Do designs effectively engage the user through strategic use of color, typography, and imagery?
- **Presentation** — How effectively is the work presented, including the templates provided, the formatting of the copy, the links shared, and the explanation of the design rationale?

**Design Scenario**


As I’m a member of the design team at a video streaming service. My task was to brainstorm ideas and design wireframes that offer a unique approach to service usability, setting it apart from other competitors.


**Project Goal**


To provide users with an intuitive and exceptional service, I've decided to introduce a new feature called "Community." Users will be able to effortlessly engage with all available content topics on the platform, fostering discussions and sharing their honest opinions. The goal is to:


- Build a robust platform user base
- Establish a platform where users can freely share their candid opinions.
