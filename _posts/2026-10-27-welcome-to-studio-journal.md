This is a sample article in the first version of the static publishing system.

## Writing without a complicated stack

Articles are stored as Markdown files with a small block of YAML metadata at the top. That makes them portable, searchable, and easy to version in Git.

You can use **bold**, *italic*, links, lists, quotations, and images.

> The dashboard we build next will let you create and edit this content in a visual editor.

### A simple list

- Draft and revise articles.
- Add categories and tags.
- Choose a cover image.
- Preview before publishing.
