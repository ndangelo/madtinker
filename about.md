MadTinker is a working notebook for ideas in progress. It brings together experiments in design, code, media, and the classroom—along with the references, questions, and processes that connect them.

The site is maintained by **Nicholas D’Angelo**, an artist and educator. Rather than presenting everything as a finished project, MadTinker makes room for testing, documenting, revisiting, and sharing work as it develops.

### What's here

**Journal** — Notes, experiments, and reflections on creative practice and technology. [Browse the journal]({{ '/archive/' | relative_url }}).

**Teaching** — Lessons, assignments, and classroom resources, including material imported from *Nugget of Joy*. Some older links, images, and embeds are still being reviewed. [Explore the teaching archive]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}).

### How it's built

MadTinker is a lightweight static site published through GitHub Pages. Its content lives in a GitHub repository, with a browser-based editing workflow through Pages CMS. The site itself is an ongoing experiment in making publishing simpler, more flexible, and more useful for creative work.

This is an evolving archive, not a finished collection.
