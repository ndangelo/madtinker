### Project Overview


For this project, you will design a **mobile travel app** that helps users plan and organize a trip from beginning to end.


Your app should allow a user to **search for and book travel, discover activities, and organize their entire trip in one place**.


You will choose an existing travel brand to redesign/reimagine **or create your own travel brand**. Your goal is not simply to make attractive screens. You must demonstrate a thoughtful **UI/UX process** that solves real problems for travelers.


**Timeline:** 1–2 weeks  
**Platform:** iOS or Android  
**Primary Tool:** Figma or another approved UI/UX design tool


## The Design Challenge


Your app should allow users to:


- Find and compare **travel deals**.
- Book a **flight**
- Book a **hotel**
- Book a **rental car**
- Find and select **activities**
- Organize plans into an **itinerary**
- Receive **notifications**

## **Deliverables**


## Research


Research at least **two existing travel apps**, including the provided examples such as KAYAK and Expedia.


Identify:


- What works well
- What could be improved
- Features you would like to incorporate into your app


Your research should influence your design decisions.


## Target Audience


Define who your app is designed for.


Consider:


- Who are your users?
- What are their travel needs?
- What problems might they have when planning a trip?


---


## Your Design Solution


Identify **three unique features or improvements** that will make your app different from existing travel apps.


For each feature, explain:


- What it does
- What user problem it solves


These features should be reflected in your final design.


---


# Required Process


You must show the development of your idea through:


1. **Research**
2. **Initial sketches**
3. **User flow**
4. **Low/High-fidelity wireframes**
5. **High-fidelity UI designs**/**Clickable prototype**


---


# Required Screens


Your final design must include, at minimum:


- **Home screen** — access to flights, hotels, cars, and activities
- **Hotel listing screen**
- **Hotel map view**
- **Itinerary screen**
- **Notification screen/experience**


Your prototype should also demonstrate the **flight, hotel, car, and activity booking experiences**.


---


# Successful Project


A successful project will demonstrate:


- Clear research and understanding of existing travel apps
- A clearly defined target user
- A clear problem being solved
- Three meaningful improvements or unique features
- A logical user flow
- Consistent and thoughtful UI design
- A functional clickable prototype
- A complete travel-planning experience


### Most Important:


**Don't just design attractive screens. Design an experience that makes planning and managing a trip easier for your user.**
